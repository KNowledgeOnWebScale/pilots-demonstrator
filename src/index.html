Automated process-based access control

Dynamic roles with Verifiable Credentials, usage control with ODRL and input validation with SHACL in the PILOTS certified container weighing process.

⚠ All verification and evaluation on this page is stubbed: signatures are fake hashes and the ODRL Evaluator and SHACL validator are simplified. Each scenario stands on its own.

  • 1. Organisational identity
  • 2. Process role
  • 3. Access evaluation

Scenario 1: Establishing organisational identity

Van Moer Logistics and CertiWeight issue affiliation credentials to their employees Alice and Bob. An employee proves the affiliation by presenting the credential in a Verifiable Presentation (VP) signed with the key of their own DID. The verifier checks both signatures, that the credential subject signed the VP, and that the issuer is the organisation claimed. No central identity provider is needed.

Scenario 2: Establishing the role within a process

A governance authority (the observer) defines which organisations may assign which process roles: Van Moer Logistics may assign dpv:ServiceConsumer, CertiWeight may assign dpv:ServiceProvider. An employee presents a role credential in a VP. On top of the signature checks, the verifier checks that the governance framework authorises the issuer to assign that role.

Scenario 3: Evaluating an access request with input validation

Alice requests the certificate of a container's weight. The ODRL Evaluator combines an Evaluation Request (who asks what, including her role), a State of the World (the purchaseCertificate process event and payment) and an ODRL Policy. The policy requires the dpv:ServiceConsumer role and requires the event to conform to a SHACL shape from the policy store. The result is an ODRL Compliance Report.
